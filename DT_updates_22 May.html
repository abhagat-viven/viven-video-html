This update brings a completely redesigned way on how you interact with your Twin. Everything now starts from one place:

A New Home

Every conversation across every Twin you interact with now lives on Home. Questions teammates asked your Twin & Experts, questions you asked your Twin or theirs, all organized by recency. Search by person or keyword to find any past exchange.

You can verify it, edit it, or chime in with context. You stay in control of how your Twin is representing you.


Train from conversations

When a conversation captures something worth keeping and sharing with more people, hit Train Now. Your Twin learns from the full thread, not just a single turn.

One good answer, refined once, becomes something your whole team can rely on forever.


First things First

Share your Twin with the person who pings you the most. Ask them to run one real question through it this week.

You'll see the exchange on your Home feed. If the answer could be better, hit Train Now from that conversation and tighten it.
