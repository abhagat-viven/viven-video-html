# People — AI-generated portraits

All faces in the film are AI-generated and fictional (guardrail 5: no photos of real people). The end card's disclosure line becomes "Dramatisation · AI-generated voices and faces".

Output: `assets/people/<id>.png`, square, 1K, cropped for circular avatars.

## Shared style (prepended to every prompt)

> Photorealistic head-and-shoulders portrait for a profile avatar. Subject centred, looking at camera with a relaxed natural expression, shoulders square to the lens. Soft natural window light from the left, gentle fill, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens look. Background softly blurred, warm neutral tones with a hint of peach and lavender, no text, no logos. Contemporary Indian college setting. Natural skin texture, no retouching gloss, no jewellery glare. Muted, warm colour grade, consistent across a set. Square frame with headroom so the face sits in the centre circle.
