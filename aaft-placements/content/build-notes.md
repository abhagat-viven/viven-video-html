# Build notes (index.html)

## Frame: always 16:9 (decided 14 Nov 2026)

The film is recorded in 16:9 on a MacBook, whose screen is 16:10.

- Every scene is laid out on one fixed **1920×1080 stage**, the same size as the storyboard frames.
- At load and on every resize, the stage scales to **fit the window**, keeping 16:9, and centres itself.
- The leftover space becomes **bars above and below** (or at the sides, on a wider window). Colour is set by URL:
  - `?bars=black` (default): clean letterbox, easy to crop.
  - `?bars=white`
  - `?bars=ground`: bars continue the sunset gradient, for a recording that's never cropped.
- The fake cursor, debug overlay and "Press Space" gate all live **inside** the stage, so they scale with it and never land in the bars.
- `cursor: none` covers the whole page, bars included.

## Audio policy (decided 14 Nov 2026)

- **The HTML plays voices only:** narrator (Ranbir), interviewer (Briggs), Riya (Zara), Arjun (Aashish), at the positions in `content/timeline.json` (the single timing file, also read by `tools/mix_preview.py` and `prototypes/soundtrack.html`). Every visual cue is relative to a beat in that file or tied to a spoken word, so retiming means editing that file only. The orb and mic react to the voices.
- **No music in the HTML.** Music bed D (`assets/music/bed_d.mp3`) is added in Canva, so its level can rise and fall by scene.
- **Music preview only:** `index.html?music=1` plays the bed under the voices at the per-scene levels in `content/timeline.json` (M toggles it). It uses `assets/music/bed_d_fit.mp3` (bed D, generated at 2:15). `?music=b|c|e` plays the other beds for comparison. Never record a take with `?music=1`.
- **Sync:** pressing Space shows a one-frame white flash with a short beep at film time 0:00. Line the music's start and `voices_master.wav` up to it.
- **Deliverables for the edit** (all in `deliverables/`, regenerated by `python3 tools/export.py` from the same timing file):
  - `voices_master.wav`: every voice on one track, 48 kHz / 24-bit, the film's full length (2:15), starting at film time 0:00 (the flash frame). Use it if you replace the recorded system audio.
  - `captions.srt`: on-screen wording (₹, digits, AAFT, TwinCafe), timed from the ElevenLabs word timings.
  - `music-cue-sheet.md`: music level per beat, hit points, and every voice region to duck under.
  - Music: `assets/music/bed_d.mp3` (bed D, 2:15, no stretch needed).

## Recording setup (Screen Studio)

1. Open the film in Chrome and go **full screen** (⌃⌘F), so no browser chrome shows.
2. In Screen Studio, choose **Record area** and snap it to the 16:9 stage, or record the full screen and crop to 16:9 in the editor. The bars make the crop edge obvious.
3. Browser zoom 100%. The stage scales itself, so don't use ⌘+/⌘−.
4. `?debug=1` shows the timecode inside the stage (bottom-left); turn it off for the real take.
