## A. "What does Deloitte test in the online round?"
Asked by: a team member · Sources pill: **3 Sources**

> Deloitte's online round tests **SQL, Python, and aptitude** for the Analyst – Data & AI role.
>
> Focus preparation on:
>
> - **SQL:** joins, aggregations, and window functions
> - **Python:** pandas-based data handling and basic analysis
> - **Aptitude:** structured problem-solving
>
> Previous candidates reported questions such as finding the top three products by revenue within each region and handling missing values in a customer dataset.

## B. "how do i prepare for deloitte online round?"
Asked by: own message (right-aligned bubble) · no Sources pill shown

> For the listed **Deloitte Analyst – Data & AI** online test, prioritise **SQL, Python, and aptitude**.
>
> - **SQL:** practise joins, `GROUP BY`, aggregations, CTEs, and especially window functions. Be able to solve ranking questions such as finding the top three products by revenue in each region.
> - **Python:** revise pandas—loading data, filtering, joins/merges, missing-value handling, grouping, and basic analysis.
> - **Aptitude:** practise timed quantitative and logical-reasoning questions; accuracy under a clock matters.
> - **Practice format:** take 2–3 timed mixed tests before the round, then review every error by topic rather than only checking the score.
>
> A practical 7-day split: 3 days SQL, 2 days Python/pandas, 1 day aptitude, and 1 full mock plus revision. Be ready to explain the logic behind each answer, since the technical interview is likely to build on the same fundamentals.

## C. "How long do I have to decline an offer?"
Sources pill: **2 Sources**

> You have 48 hours from the result to decline an offer, and the decline must be submitted in writing to your CRM. After that, the offer is treated as accepted.

## D. "What was the median package last year?"
Sources pill: **1 Source**
